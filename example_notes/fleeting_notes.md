# Fleeting Notes

Fleeting notes capture thoughts as they occur, wherever you are. Their purpose is
to prevent an idea from being forgotten before you have time to process it.

Use them as temporary reminders. Review and process them within two days, then
delete the temporary notes. Their value depends on this follow-up: simply
collecting thoughts without processing them does not make them useful.

## Source

Sönke Ahrens, *How to Take Smart Notes*.
