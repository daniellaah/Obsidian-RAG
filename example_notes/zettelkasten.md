# Zettelkasten

Zettelkasten is a note-taking method associated with Niklas Luhmann. This workflow
uses three types of notes:

- [Fleeting notes](fleeting_notes.md) capture thoughts that occur during the day.
- [Literature notes](literature_notes.md) record ideas drawn from reading.
- [Permanent notes](permanent_notes.md) develop ideas by connecting and organizing
  material from the other two types.

The process moves from capturing thoughts and recording knowledge to examining
how those ideas relate to one another. Permanent notes hold the results of that
work.
